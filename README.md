## 👋 About Me

I'm an **IT Officer at HDFC Bank's NOC / Command Centre in Airoli, Navi Mumbai**, where I work with the **CITOQ** team on incident escalations, alert monitoring, and planned maintenance activities — keeping critical banking systems observable and accountable around the clock.

Alongside that, I'm **co-founder of [Currikit](mailto:currikit.edu@gmail.com)**, an academic services and web development studio based in Navi Mumbai, where I build websites, learning material, and tooling for students and small businesses.

I'm currently completing a **Minor in AI & Data Science from IIT Mandi (CCE program)**, sharpening SQL, Python, and BI skills through real projects rather than tutorials.

My goal: transition into a **Data Analyst / Business Analyst** role where I can turn messy operational data into decisions people actually act on. ⚡
